# RUNTONG & WAYEAH — Supply Chain Model

## Operating model
RUNTONG & WAYEAH operates through a trade-and-manufacturing-resource integration model supported by long-term partner factories and suppliers.

The company is not represented as owning every factory used for every product.

## General supply-chain capabilities
Internal source material states that the company has:
- 50+ long-term factory resources,
- multiple partner factories with relationships exceeding 5 or 10 years,
- supplier matching based on product, price, certification, lead time, and order volume,
- support for both smaller trial orders and large-volume orders when the applicable product/factory permits,
- OEM/ODM development support,
- mold-development coordination,
- pre-production sample confirmation,
- production-progress follow-up,
- pre-shipment inspection,
- customer factory-audit coordination when applicable.

## Company value in the supply chain
The company combines supplier resources with:
- customer communication,
- product-development coordination,
- sourcing and purchasing,
- quality control,
- packaging customization,
- production follow-up,
- export execution and delivery coordination.

## Correct external positioning
Preferred concept:
`long-term manufacturing partners + sourcing/integration + development + QC + export execution`

Avoid broad statements such as:
`all products are manufactured in our own factories`

unless a specific project has confirmed that the relevant production site is in fact self-owned and the statement is authorized.

## Project-specific confirmation rule
Before making a customer-facing commitment, confirm the actual factory/supplier for the project where relevant to:
- MOQ,
- production process,
- material minimum,
- tooling,
- certification,
- audit status,
- testing,
- lead time,
- production capacity,
- packaging constraints.

## Fact scope
This file is `COMPANY_GENERAL`. Individual factory capability is `FACTORY_SPECIFIC` and must be stored or confirmed separately.
