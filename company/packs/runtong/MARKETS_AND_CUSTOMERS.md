# RUNTONG & WAYEAH — Markets, Customer Types and Acquisition Channels

## Target market orientation
The company's main B2B export focus includes Europe, North America and Latin America, with additional business across other international markets.

### Europe
Typical emphasis: quality, certification, packaging and mid/high-end positioning.
Relevant customer types: pharmacy/drugstore chains, brands, supermarkets and distributors.

### North America
Typical emphasis: product positioning, retail maturity and supply-chain stability.
Relevant customer types: brands, wholesalers, distributors and supermarkets.

### Latin America
Typical emphasis: growth opportunity, price sensitivity and distributor/retail fit.
Relevant customer types: supermarkets, chain stores, importers and distributors.

### Other markets in the source material
Japan/Korea, Middle East, South Africa and Australia are also identified as relevant markets with different quality, pricing, packaging and channel expectations.

## Main customer/account types
- brand owners,

## Offline acquisition channels
Examples include:
- Canton Fair,
- China and overseas trade shows,
- overseas customer visits,
- Europe/Americas field development,
- retail/channel field research.

Offline work is not only for lead collection. It may also be used for competitor collection, price-band research, packaging observation, channel-demand assessment and relationship building.

## Prospecting implication
When using the `prospecting` skill, prioritize channel fit and product-fit logic over generic company introduction. Recommendations should reflect what the account visibly sells or lacks rather than defaulting to the full catalogue.

## Boundary
Market/channel descriptions are general positioning guidance. Do not infer a specific prospect's purchasing volume, supplier setup, private-label activity or decision authority without evidence.
