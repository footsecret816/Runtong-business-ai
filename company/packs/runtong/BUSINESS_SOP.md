# RUNTONG & WAYEAH — Business SOP and Internal Responsibility Map

## Purpose
Provide company-specific workflow context for project analysis and next-action planning. This is a general SOP map, not proof that a specific action has already been completed.

## 1. Inquiry stage
Typical actions:
- record inquiry source,
- determine customer/account ownership,
- conduct prospect/customer research,
- clarify customer requirements,
- determine whether the product is core, extended-project, or requires external sourcing,
- determine whether management involvement is needed.

Primary internal roles: sales / business team, with management involvement where needed.

## 2. Quotation and sampling stage
Typical actions:
- confirm product image/reference, quantity, packaging, material, size, function and other requirements,
- obtain sourcing/factory cost input,
- prepare quotation,
- identify whether price requires management approval,
- confirm sample cost where applicable,
- arrange sample development,
- ship samples and preserve records.

Primary roles: sales + purchasing/sourcing + design where packaging/artwork is involved; management for approval-sensitive pricing.

## 3. Order / contract stage
Typical actions:
- confirm order details,
- prepare PI,
- confirm payment terms,
- confirm deposit/payment receipt where required,
- enter order into internal system,
- issue purchasing/production instructions,
- hand off to purchasing.

Primary roles: sales + finance + purchasing.

## 4. Purchasing / production execution
Typical actions:
- confirm supplier/factory lead time,
- confirm pre-production sample where required,
- arrange production,
- follow production progress,
- coordinate abnormalities or deviations.

Primary roles: purchasing/sourcing + sales; management may be involved in major exceptions.

## 5. Production inspection / QC
Typical actions:
- mid-production or pre-shipment checks when applicable,
- photo/document retention,
- inspection reporting,
- classify nonconformities,
- escalate material issues for management decision when needed.

Primary roles: QC + purchasing + sales.

## 6. Shipment / documentation
Typical actions:
- confirm balance payment where applicable,
- booking / warehouse entry,
- customs declaration,
- shipment departure,
- bill of lading and shipping documents,
- document archiving,
- export-tax-refund documentation preparation.

Primary roles: sales + finance + documentation/shipping team.

## 7. After-sales / settlement / learning
Typical actions:
- collect customer receipt/quality feedback,
- record after-sales issues,
- trace supplier responsibility where needed,
- reconcile and settle accounts,
- conduct project review,
- capture durable customer/project knowledge.

Primary roles: sales + purchasing + QC + finance as relevant.

## Team responsibility reference
- Sales / business: customer communication, inquiry response, quotation, sampling follow-up, order confirmation, shipment coordination, relationship maintenance.
- Purchasing: supplier communication, cost checking, sampling, production follow-up, exception coordination, factory information collection.
- QC: inspection, production checks, corrective follow-up, inspection reports, final quality confirmation.
- Operations/marketing: B2B platforms, website, SEO, social media and data/keyword support.
- Design: packaging, product graphics, catalogue, customer artwork and exhibition materials.
- Market/product: product planning, market research, competitor analysis, product-solution development.
- Finance: receipts/payments, reconciliation, invoices, tax-refund support and payment-risk control.
- Documentation/shipping: export documents, customs, bill of lading, archiving.
- Management: strategic decisions, key-customer judgment, critical pricing approval, major exceptions and supply-chain risk decisions.

## AI usage rule
When `project-next-action` proposes next steps, use this map to suggest likely responsible functions only when useful. Do not claim an internal person has accepted, approved, scheduled or completed an action unless confirmed.
