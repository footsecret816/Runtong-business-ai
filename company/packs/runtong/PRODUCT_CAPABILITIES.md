- shoe trees,

Representative categories include knee, wrist, elbow and ankle supports, sports wraps/support bands, cold/hot therapy products, instant cold packs, hot-water bottles, and recovery-related items.

## Insole material capability overview
Common materials include PU foam, EVA, TPU support components, TPE/SEBS, Gel / PU Gel, silicone, Poron, memory foam, latex, recycled foam materials, carbon plate, PE foam, leather, mesh, fleece, towel fabric, sandwich fabric, and cooling-type fabrics.
