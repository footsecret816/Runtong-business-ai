# RUNTONG & WAYEAH — Insole Knowledge

## Functional categories
Representative insole directions include:
- Comfort Insole — daily comfort, soft cushioning, fatigue reduction.
- Support Insole — arch support, stability and load-distribution support.
- Work Insole — cushioning and pressure relief for prolonged standing / work footwear.
- Active / Sport Insole — sports cushioning, stability and anti-slip support.
- Pain-Relief Positioned Insole — product concepts around plantar-fascia, heel or forefoot discomfort; claims require caution.
- 3/4 Insole — partial-length support for narrower or formal shoes.
- Leather Insole — leather-based comfort / moisture-management positioning for dress footwear.
- Children Insole — children's daily comfort / light-support positioning.
- ESD Insole — conductive / anti-static applications for relevant work environments.
- Diabetic-Positioned Insole — soft pressure-reduction positioning for sensitive feet; medical claims require caution.

## Risk boundaries
- Do not turn product-positioning names such as `orthotic`, `diabetic`, `pain relief`, or `plantar fasciitis` into unsupported treatment/medical claims.
- Material availability, hardness, density, tooling, dimensions, color, structure, certification, MOQ, price and lead time remain project/factory-specific.
- A reference structure or competitor sample may raise IP/tooling issues; use `risk-guard` where relevant.
