# RUNTONG & WAYEAH — Foot Care, Shoe Accessories and Sports-Support Knowledge

## Foot-care small products
Representative categories include:
- SEBS toe separators,
- bunion / toe-correction products,
- toe protectors,
- forefoot pads,
- heel cushions,
- corn pads,
- silicone cushions,
- arch pads,
- sock/sleeve-type care products,
- pressure-relief pads.

Typical channels include pharmacy/drugstore, foot-care brands, supermarkets, distributors, wholesalers and personal-care channels.

## Shoe accessories
Representative categories include:
- conventional shoelaces,
- elastic no-tie laces,
- waxed laces,
- round laces,
- flat laces,
- safety-oriented laces,
- shoehorns,
- shoe trees,
- shoe shields,
- shoe/display accessories.

These can support standalone sales or cross-category bundles with insoles and shoe-care products.

## Sports-support expansion
Representative categories include:
- knee supports,
- wrist supports,
- elbow supports,
- ankle supports,
- sports wraps/bandages,
- support straps,
- hot/cold therapy items,
- instant cold packs,
- recovery-related products.

This category is an expansion direction and can connect naturally with sports insoles, foot care and recovery solutions.

## Hot-water / thermal-care expansion
Representative items include:
- rubber hot-water bottles,
- long-format hot-water bottles,
- neck-shaped hot-water bottles,
- hot/cold packs,
- eye-cooling products,
- sports-injury hot/cold therapy products.

## Cross-category opportunity logic
Potential assortment combinations may include:
- sports insole + ankle/knee support,
- foot-care pads + support insoles,
- shoe-care + laces + insole bundles,
- recovery / hot-cold products for pharmacy or sports channels.

Use such combinations as opportunity ideas, not as proof that a specific customer wants the full range.

## Risk boundaries
- Corrective, pain-relief, diabetic, recovery, injury or medical-sounding positioning requires careful claim control.
- Material composition, hardness, skin-contact requirements, test standards, regulatory classification, MOQ, factory and certification are project-specific.
- Do not represent expansion categories as mature/core capability at the same confidence level as long-established shoe-care / insole / foot-care business unless evidence supports the specific claim.
