# RUNTONG & WAYEAH — Shoe Care Knowledge

## Core shoe-care categories

### Shoe polish
Representative forms:
- solid polish,
- liquid polish,
- neutral / colored polish,
- sponge-head liquid polish,
- canned polish.

Common positioning:
- shine,
- color refresh,
- leather conditioning,
- anti-drying / daily maintenance,
- quick-use care.

### Shoe brushes
Representative forms:
- wooden-handle,
- plastic,
- horsehair,
- bristle,
- nylon,
- suede,
- double-sided,
- cleaning,
- polishing,
- travel brushes.

### Shoe shine sponges
Representative directions:
- conventional shine sponge,
- reservoir/oil-containing designs,
- round/square formats,
- travel formats.

### Sneaker cleaners
Representative formats:
- foam cleaner,
- liquid cleaner,
- gel cleaner,
- brush-head foam cleaner,
- refill,
- cleaning wipes,
- cleaning kits.

Common kit combinations include cleaner + brush, cleaner + cloth, wipes + brush, foam + refill, or fuller sneaker-cleaning sets.

### Cleaning / polishing cloths
Representative materials/forms:
- cotton,
- microfiber,
- polishing/fleece-type cloth,
- nonwoven,
- disposable cleaning cloth,
- double-sided care cloth.

### Shoehorns
Representative forms:
- plastic,
- metal,
- wood,
- long/short handle,
- portable,
- hanging,
- gift-oriented.

### Shoe trees
Representative forms:
- wood,
- plastic,
- spring structures,
- aromatic wood,
- beech,
- adjustable,
- boot trees.

### Shoe repair products
Examples:
- shoe glue,
- color-restoration products,
- repair patches,
- heel patches,
- anti-slip sole pads,
- sole repair patches,
- leather-repair accessories.

### Deodorizing / fragrance products
Examples:
- deodorizer balls,
- wood/fragrance bags,
- shoe deodorizer bags,
- activated-carbon bags,
- fragrance sheets,
- shoe sprays.

### Shoe-care kits
Representative combinations:
- polish + brush + cloth,
- cleaner + brush + cloth,
- foam + refill,
- wipes + brush,
- polish + shine sponge + polishing cloth,
- travel-care sets.

## Common customization dimensions
Depending on product and factory:
- logo printing / laser marking,
- handle or product color,
- labels,
- bottle/can graphics,
- package/carton/display-box design,
- kit combination,
- volume/size,
- product structure or mold where feasible.

## Channel-fit examples
- supermarkets / general retail,
- shoe stores / shoe brands,
- shoe-care brands,
- sports/sneaker channels,
- online brands,
- wholesale/distribution,
- hotel/travel/gift channels for selected items.

## Boundary
Exact formulation, chemical compliance, material, brush hair type, capacity, packaging count, MOQ, certification, price, lead time and factory must be confirmed at project level. Do not generalize one shoe-care product's compliance documents to another.
