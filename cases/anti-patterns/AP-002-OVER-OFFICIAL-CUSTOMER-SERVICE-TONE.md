# AP-002 — Over-Official Customer-Service Tone

## Failure pattern
The AI turns ordinary B2B communication into a formal corporate-service message: repeated gratitude, ceremonial wording, long introductions, and polished but emotionally distant phrasing.

## Typical symptoms
- `We are pleased to inform you...` for routine updates.
- Repeating company background to an existing customer.
- Multiple sentences of thanks before reaching the actual business point.
- Generic service phrases that could apply to any customer or industry.
- Long closings after a simple operational update.

## Why it fails
- Slows down real business communication.
- Makes established relationships feel unnatural.
- Hides the actual decision or update.
- Sounds generated rather than commercially experienced.

## Preferred correction
Use relationship-sensitive professionalism:
- new prospect → brief credibility + clear relevance,
- existing customer → direct update + required action,
- negotiation → reason + position + workable next step,
- technical issue → precise facts + decision needed.

## Rule
Professional does not mean ceremonial. Use the minimum formality needed to maintain trust and clarity.
