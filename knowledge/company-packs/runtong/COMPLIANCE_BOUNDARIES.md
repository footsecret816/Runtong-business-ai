# RUNTONG & WAYEAH — Compliance and Certification Boundaries

## General document capability
Depending on product, factory and project, the company may be able to provide or coordinate documents such as:
- ISO 9001,
- BSCI,
- FDA-related documentation where applicable,
- CE where applicable,
- SGS reports,
- MSDS,
- RoHS,
- product test reports,
- material declarations,
- factory information,
- shipment/export documentation.

## Mandatory scope rule
Never assume that one certificate applies across all products, factories or projects.

Before customer-facing use, verify:
1. which product the document applies to,
2. which factory/site it applies to,
3. whether it is current and valid,
4. whether the customer's intended use requires a specific report form or issuing body,
5. whether the claim being made is actually supported by the document.

## Factory audit boundary
If a customer may audit the production site, confirm the actual production factory and its audit readiness before making any commitment.

## Supply-chain document risk
If a project would rely on documentation from another supply-chain entity, treat this as a risk requiring explicit review. Do not present borrowed or unrelated documentation as if it directly covered the active product/factory.

## Functional / medical-claim boundary
For functional items such as orthotic-positioned, diabetic-positioned, pain-relief, plantar-fasciitis or corrective foot-care products, avoid unsupported medical or treatment claims. Product marketing language must be matched to actual evidence and target-market requirements.

## IP boundary
For competitor references, character/IP designs, patented structures, or near-identical appearance requests, assess IP risk before promising 1:1 replication. Prefer functional/visual direction matching without copying protected expression when risk is unclear.

## Regulatory conclusion boundary
This company pack does not itself establish legal or regulatory acceptance. Formal compliance conclusions may require verified current regulations, laboratories, certification bodies, or legal/regulatory professionals.

## Fact scope
The listed document types are `COMPANY_GENERAL` possibilities only. Availability for the active project remains `TO_CONFIRM` unless project-specific evidence exists.
