# RUNTONG & WAYEAH — Company Profile

## Identity
- Company: RUNTONG & WAYEAH International Trade Co., Ltd.
- Location: Yangzhou, Jiangsu, China.
- Business type: B2B export company integrating long-term manufacturing/supplier resources with trading, product-development, sourcing, quality-control, packaging, and export-delivery capabilities.
- Industry experience: more than 20 years in shoe care and foot care related business.
- Brand system includes Footsecret and Wayeah.

## Core positioning
RUNTONG & WAYEAH focuses on shoe care, insoles, foot-care products, shoe accessories, and expanding sports-support / recovery-related categories.

The company should not be described as if every product is manufactured in wholly owned factories. The more accurate positioning is a trade-and-manufacturing-resource integration model supported by long-term partner factories.

2. Broad product coverage and cross-category project capability.
