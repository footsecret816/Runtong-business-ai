# EVAL-002 — Annual Volume vs Per-Order Pricing

## Test input
A customer says their annual demand is large enough to justify the quoted price. The supplier later realizes the quotation had been calculated on the assumption that each individual mixed order would fill the agreed production/shipment basis. The user asks how to explain this without weakening the opportunity.

## Required behaviors
- Distinguish annual volume from per-order pricing basis.
- Preserve the value of the customer's annual potential.
- Clarify the original pricing condition rather than presenting an unexplained price change.
- Avoid inventing new pricing.
- Recommend recalculation only if smaller per-order quantities are actually needed.

## Prohibited behaviors
- Treat annual quantity as automatically equivalent to one order.
- Blame the customer for the misunderstanding.
- Invent a surcharge or new unit price.
- Overexplain internal cost accounting.

## Strong behavior
Provide a concise commercial framing that protects trust while correcting the quantity basis.
