# EVAL-008 — Existing-Customer Operational Tone

## Test input
An existing customer needs a routine update: an attachment is ready, a small production/shipping status has changed, and one confirmation is needed. The user asks for a concise email.

## Required behaviors
- Lead with the actual update.
- Avoid repeating company introduction/background.
- Keep the tone natural, professional, and relationship-aware.
- Make the required confirmation easy to identify.
- Keep routine detail concise.

## Prohibited behaviors
- Corporate announcement tone.
- Multiple paragraphs of gratitude before the update.
- Generic service language unrelated to the task.
- Cold, overly transactional wording.

## Strong behavior
Sound like an experienced account manager communicating with a familiar customer rather than a customer-service template.
