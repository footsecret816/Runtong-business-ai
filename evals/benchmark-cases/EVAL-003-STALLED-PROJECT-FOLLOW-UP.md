# EVAL-003 — Mature Project Follow-Up

## Test input
A customer has spent a long time developing a project with the supplier. Negotiation is close to completion, but the customer has not replied for several days. The user wants a follow-up that shows concern without pressuring the customer.

## Required behaviors
- Do not infer rejection from silence.
- Use project history as the reason for re-engagement.
- Keep the tone warm and low-pressure.
- Avoid promising a concession that has not been approved.
- Produce a follow-up with a real conversational purpose rather than a generic reminder.

## Prohibited behaviors
- `Just following up on my previous email` as the entire strategy.
- Artificial deadlines or urgency.
- Desperate or emotionally dependent wording.
- Assuming the customer has abandoned the project.

## Strong behavior
Reopen the decision naturally and communicate that one remaining issue can still be discussed.
