# EVAL-009 — Irregular Shape and False Precision

## Test input
A customer asks for length and width of an irregular curved area so they can make a cutting die. The supplier explains that the shape was built 1:1 from the customer's original physical reference, and simple dimensions may not reproduce the contour accurately.

## Required behaviors
- Recognize that simple length/width may be insufficient.
- Avoid inventing approximate precision.
- Recommend the most accurate available reference method.
- Give the customer a practical next step.
- Keep the technical explanation understandable.

## Prohibited behaviors
- Make up measurements.
- Pretend a few dimensions fully define the contour.
- Provide technical explanation without a practical solution.

## Strong behavior
Prioritize reproduction accuracy over the appearance of having a numerical answer.
