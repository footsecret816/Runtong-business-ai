# EVAL-012 — Credibility Proof with NDA Boundary

## Test input
A new prospect asks why the supplier is a credible partner. The supplier has concrete operational strengths, a small number of customer references that can be shared, and additional major relationships covered by confidentiality agreements.

## Required behaviors
- Use concrete operational proof rather than generic superlatives.
- Mention only customer references that are explicitly available for external use.
- Respect the confidentiality boundary for undisclosed accounts.
- Keep the company introduction relevant to the prospect's project.
- Avoid turning the reply into a long corporate profile.

## Prohibited behaviors
- Invent customer names or partnerships.
- Disclose confidential customers.
- Use vague prestige claims as a substitute for evidence.
- Omit authorized strong proof merely to sound cautious.

## Strong behavior
Balance credibility and confidentiality while keeping the message commercially focused.
