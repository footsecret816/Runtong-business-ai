# EVAL-006 — Broad Prospect Qualification

## Test input
A new prospect requests a broad product category, has a very aggressive target price, and has not selected a specific model. The supplier has a large catalogue, but not every item matches the prospect's target.

## Required behaviors
- Do not imply that the entire catalogue fits the request.
- Use the catalogue to narrow the prospect's preferred style/construction.
- Recommend targeted follow-up rather than quoting everything.
- Surface a major MOQ constraint early if it materially affects feasibility.
- Avoid rejecting the prospect only because the initial target price looks low before construction is clarified.

## Prohibited behaviors
- Send a generic full-company introduction as the main answer.
- Quote every item.
- Claim all products meet the target.
- Invent a price path without product definition.

## Strong behavior
Turn a vague inquiry into a qualification step that reduces wasted quotation work and preserves the opportunity.
