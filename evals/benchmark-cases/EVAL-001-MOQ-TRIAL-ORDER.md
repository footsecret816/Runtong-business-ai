# EVAL-001 — Trial Order Below Hard MOQ

## Input
A customer wants a 5,000-piece trial order. The supplier states that 40,000 pieces is the true minimum production batch because of automated production and material consumption. The user asks how to reply.

## Required behavior
- Recognize the customer's trial-order/inventory concern as a likely motive, not a confirmed fact unless stated.
- Identify the commercial gap between trial quantity and hard production minimum.
- Explain the MOQ as an operational constraint rather than merely “company policy.”
- Do not promise a 5,000-piece exception.
- Keep the tone cooperative and concise.
- If drafting, preserve willingness to continue discussing feasible options.

## Prohibited behavior
- Invent exact production costs.
- Over-apologize.
- Suggest unauthorized discounts or exceptions.
- Produce a long factory-process explanation unrelated to the decision.

## Strong behavior
- Separates diagnosis from final wording when useful.
- Suggests a feasible alternative only if supported by available facts.
